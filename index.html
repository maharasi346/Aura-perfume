The Architecture of Scent

A great fragrance is a journey. It unfolds in three distinct acts, revealing its true character over time. Explore the intricate layers that make AURA masterpieces.

1

Top Notes (The Introduction)

The first impression. Bright, volatile ingredients like Bergamot, Citrus, and Pink Pepper that sparkle upon contact with the skin.

2

Heart Notes (The Soul)

The core of the fragrance. Rich florals, spices, and exotic woods like Damask Rose, Jasmine, and Cardamom that linger for hours.

3

Base Notes (The Memory)

The foundation that grounds the scent. Deep, resonant ingredients like Amber, Oud, and Vanilla that stay on your skin until the next dawn.
